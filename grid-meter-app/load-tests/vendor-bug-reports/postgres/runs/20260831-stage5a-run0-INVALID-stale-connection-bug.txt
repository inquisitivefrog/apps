methodology bug, not a real HA finding. Do not cite this as "no self-protection."

Mechanism used: /etc/hosts blackhole on patroni-3 (redirect consul-3 to an
unroutable IP) -- WITHOUT also restarting the target Consul agent. This
was later found to have no effect on Patroni's already-open, pooled
